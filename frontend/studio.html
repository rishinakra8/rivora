Our Story

Design with intention. Built with discipline.

Rivora Design Studio is a Gurgaon-based interior design and build practice creating distinctive residential, hospitality, and commercial spaces across india and serving globally.

Rivora was born from a personal passion for design and a belief in doing things with honesty, trust, and intention.

For us, design is more than creating beautiful spaces. It is about creating something distinctive — something that has character, creates an impression, and becomes a meaningful part of the lives of the people who experience it. We are drawn to luxury not simply as a statement of material or scale, but as a reflection of thoughtful detailing, quality, craftsmanship, and individuality.

We believe that a great project is created when good design and good execution are given equal importance. A beautiful concept means little if it cannot be translated faithfully into reality, and exceptional execution cannot compensate for a design without purpose.

That is why we approach every project from concept to completion with the same level of care. We design with a clear vision, develop every detail with intention, and execute with precision — ensuring that what is imagined is brought to life as it was meant to be.

At the heart of Rivora is a simple philosophy: be honest, build trust, create exceptional design, and deliver it with integrity.

Our work spans residential, hospitality, and commercial interiors, including ground-up interior fit-outs and large-format spaces such as Sesh Sports N Fitness in Gurgaon.

Rivora is where passion for design meets the discipline of execution — creating spaces that are unique, enduring, and made to leave an impact.

Ar. Rishi Nakra
Founder & Principal Architect
Studio Ethos

Principles that guide every drawing and site decision.

The foundation of how we create spaces that endure through time.

01

Architect-Led Rigour

Designers who understand construction drawings, structural loads, and services integration — so creative ideas never collapse in execution.

02

Single Point of Accountability

No finger-pointing between designers and contractors. We own the drawing, the procurement, the labour, and the outcome.

03

Transparent BOQ & Timelines

Fixed bill-of-quantities with material specifications locked before civil work starts. Clear milestones, no surprise variations.

04

Enduring Materiality

Materials chosen for how they age, not just how they look on handover day. Quality detailing that holds up to daily life.

How We Work

One studio, four stages, zero handoffs.

Because design and construction sit under one roof, every stage below is delivered by the same team that drew the first sketch.

01

Concept & Brief

Site study, brief and budget alignment, followed by spatial planning and a design direction — mood, material and layout — presented for sign-off before a single drawing is finalised.

02

Design & BOQ Documentation

Detailed working drawings, 3D visualisation, and a fixed Bill of Quantities covering civil, MEP, furniture and finishes — so the final cost is known before construction begins.

03

Site Execution & Supervison

In-house execution with dedicated site supervision, weekly progress reviews and quality checks at every stage — from structural changes to the last coat of paint.

04

Snag-Free Handover

Snag-free handover with complete documentation, warranty terms and a maintenance guide — the space is move-in ready, exactly as designed.
