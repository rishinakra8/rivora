Ask A Question
Ask A Question

What does Rivora's Turnkey Execution service include?

Our turnkey service manages your project from the initial architectural drawings and 3D renders all the way to civil execution, MEP, custom millwork, procurement, and handover. You work with a single dedicated team with locked BOQs and guaranteed timelines.

How do you handle budget transparency and cost estimates?

Before starting site execution, we prepare a line-itemized Bill of Quantities (BOQ) with material specifications, brands, and rates. Once approved, rates are locked so you have absolute predictability throughout construction.

Do you take on Plotting & Master Planning projects?

Yes. We specialize in large-scale plotting layouts, sports clubs (such as our 2.8 Acre Sesh Sports project), and mixed-use commercial developments across Delhi NCR, Haryana, Rajasthan, and nationwide.

Can we collaborate on architectural design & 3D renders only?

Absolutely. We regularly provide standalone architectural blueprints, facade elevation modeling, and high-resolution 3D CGI visualisation for clients and developers globally.
