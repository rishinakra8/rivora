What We Stand On

Four convictions, held on every site.

Interior design is a promise made in a drawing and kept on a construction site. These are the terms we hold ourselves to on every project, regardless of scale.

I.

Design-Build Integrity

One team owns the drawing and the site, so nothing is lost in translation between architect and contractor.

II.

Material Honesty

We specify materials for how they age, not just how they photograph — stone, wood and metal chosen to earn a patina.

III.

Restraint Over Trend

A room should still feel considered a decade from now. We design for the client's life, not the current moodboard.

IV.

Transparent Execution

Fixed BOQs, tracked timelines and open costing — you see exactly what is being built and what it costs.

How We Work

One studio, four stages, zero handoffs.

Because design and construction sit under one roof, every stage below is delivered by the same team that drew the first sketch.

01

Concept

Site study, brief and budget alignment, followed by spatial planning and a design direction — mood, material and layout — presented for sign-off before a single drawing is finalised.

02

Design

Detailed working drawings, 3D visualisation, and a fixed Bill of Quantities covering civil, MEP, furniture and finishes — so the final cost is known before construction begins.

03

Build

In-house execution with dedicated site supervision, weekly progress reviews and quality checks at every stage — from structural changes to the last coat of paint.

04

Handover

Snag-free handover with complete documentation, warranty terms and a maintenance guide — the space is move-in ready, exactly as designed.

About The Studio

Architecture-led. Build-capable. Detail-obsessed.

Rivora Design Studio is a Gurgaon-based interior design and build practice working across residential, hospitality and commercial spaces in the NCR region. We were founded on a simple frustration with the industry norm — designers who hand off to contractors they don't control, and contractors who build without the designer's eye on site.

Rivora closes that gap. Our team designs, details and builds under one roof, which means the finish you approve on a drawing is the finish you get on site — down to the millimetre.

Our portfolio includes ground-up interior fit-outs and large-format builds such as Sesh Sports N Fitness in Gurgaon, alongside a growing slate of residential and hospitality interiors across the region.
